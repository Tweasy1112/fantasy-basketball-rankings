Data and scoring

Standard: sum of fantasy points across played games; average divided by games played. High Score: sum of each player's best game per fantasy week; average divided by weeks with at least one game. Players count regardless of fantasy roster status. Multi-position players appear under every eligible position once Yahoo eligibility is mapped. Position filters will be incomplete until that mapping is added.

League scoring: PTS + REB + 2×AST + 3×STL + 3×BLK − TO + 2×DD. A double-double means 10+ in at least two of points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks.

Import format: CSV columns date,player_id,player,team,positions,pts,reb,ast,stl,blk,tov. Optional week (recommended; exact Yahoo fantasy-week number) and dd. Without Yahoo week dates, Monday–Sunday ISO weeks are used as a provisional approximation. Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD. Only include played games, not DNPs.
